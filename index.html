Our story

Our story began with our first-year microcontroller project. When we started, none of us knew anything about drones. We had a dream and a proposal, and we had to bring them into the real world.

Here you can learn about us and how we built it. We are Team MicroSolutions. After the project, we started an R&D startup called AMD Labs.

Honest status

Where the drone stands today

This is a working research prototype and enterprise level is still our goal. We publish what works, what is limited and what still needs proving, because that is what helps the next builder.

See the challenges
DemonstratedCustom flight firmware with a 250 Hz control loop, sensor fusion and failsafes running on the STM32H743.
DemonstratedStable vertical liftoff, reached after about twelve months of testing, rig work and PID tuning.
DemonstratedAeronexus dashboard: live telemetry, live PID changes, saved gains and 3D magnetometer calibration.
DemonstratedCustom two-joystick transmitter with a radio link of about 100 m in clear line of sight.
LimitedSustained controlled hover in manual flight is not achieved yet. Thumb joysticks caused over-correction, so better gimbals are the next step.
LimitedMetal detection works as a first sweep. It finds the rough zone of a magnetic disturbance, not an exact point, depth or shape.
In codeGPS position hold, return-to-launch and waypoint survey missions are written in the firmware. We have not yet published flight results for them.
Needs fixingBattery monitoring: the firmware's reading is fixed at 13.2 V, so the dashboard value is not real and the low-battery failsafe cannot trigger.
Needs dataFormal detection tests, with known objects and measured noise levels in flight, are still to be recorded.
