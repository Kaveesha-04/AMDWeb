Build notes

The project changed as it went, so some documents describe earlier versions. This site follows the code in the repository where the sources differ. Check your own board too.

TopicThis site usesOlder references
IMUMPU6500 driverSome documents and the cost list say MPU9250
Joystick converterADS1115 at 860 samples/sSome documents say ADS1015
Barometer busSPI2Some documents say I2C
Motor spinM1 and M4 CCW, M2 and M3 CW, from the firmware mixerSome documents say M1 and M3 CCW
Telemetry UART on ESP32GPIO 17 RX, GPIO 5 TXSome documents say D17 and D18. GPIO 18 now drives the LEDs
Control loop250 HzSome documents mention much higher rates
Telemetry rate5 HzSome documents say 50 Hz
Check the tilt test against physics

The tuning guide in the repository says that when you tilt right, the left motors should speed up. A correcting drone must speed up the low side. Use the test in stage 10.

Troubleshooting

ProblemLikely causeWhat to do
Drone flips on takeoffMotor order wrong, or propellers on the wrong motorsRecheck stage 3 and stage 10's direction test, props off
Motors speed up on the wrong sideMotor order or mixing wrongStop. Do not fit propellers until fixed
Fast buzzing, hot motorsP or D too highLower P, or lower D if the motors run hot
Slow rocking at 1 to 2 HzI too highLower I
Bouncing after a correctionD too lowRaise D
Drifts with sticks centredI too low, CG off or unequal motorsCheck CG, compare motor RPM, then raise I
Oscillation only at high throttleTPA not lowering gains enoughAdjust TPA in firmware
Altitude bounces in altitude holdAltitude P too high or D too lowLower P or raise D, and check the barometer's foam cover
I2C freezes during spin-upPower rail noiseAdd capacitors, separate wiring and check the buck converter
Compass heading wandersMotor or power cable interferenceRaise the mast, twist wires and recalibrate
Gains lost after power cycleNot saved to flashSend the save command while disarmed
Telemetry not reaching dashboardWrong Wi-Fi settings or server IPRerun the Aeronexus-Setup step and check your firewall allows TCP port 5000
Dashboard always shows 13.2 VThe firmware's battery value is hardcodedExpected in current code. See the battery warning in stage 4

Drone rules

Rules on registration, permitted areas and height change over time and differ by country. Before any outdoor flight, check the current rules from your national civil aviation authority. In Sri Lanka, that is the Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lanka. Stay clear of people, roads, airports and private property without permission.

Glossary

AHRSAttitude and Heading Reference System: works out the drone's orientation from its sensors.
AirModeKeeps motors above idle so the PID loop can still steer at low throttle.
BLDCBrushless DC motor.
Buck converterAn efficient circuit that steps voltage down.
CGCentre of gravity.
CW / CCWClockwise and counter-clockwise.
Decoupling capacitorA small energy store near a chip that smooths its power supply.
ESCElectronic Speed Controller: drives a brushless motor from a control signal.
FeedforwardA PID add-on that reacts directly to stick movement.
GeofenceA distance limit that triggers a safety response.
Hard-iron distortionA constant magnetic offset caused by the drone's own parts.
Hall-effect gimbalA joystick that senses position with magnets, with no sliding contacts.
HDOPA GPS quality number. Lower is better.
IMUInertial Measurement Unit: a gyroscope plus an accelerometer.
KVMotor speed in RPM per volt, with no load.
LiPoLithium polymer battery. Powerful, and needs careful handling.
Mahony filterThe sensor fusion method used to combine gyro and accelerometer data.
PIDProportional, Integral, Derivative: the core feedback control method.
PIOPilot-induced oscillation: over-corrections by the pilot that make motion worse.
PWMPulse Width Modulation: here, a 1000 to 2000 µs pulse that sets ESC speed.
QuaternionA four-number way to represent rotation without gimbal lock.
RTLReturn to launch.
TPAThrottle PID Attenuation: lowers P and D at high throttle to prevent oscillation.
WindupWhen the I term grows too large while the output is at its limit.
